# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Android developers, indie makers, Flutter/React Native engineers, and mobile app founders who need to produce high-converting, policy-compliant Play Store and App Store screenshots quickly without wrestling bloated design tools like Canva or Photoshop.

## Product Purpose

Aperlo eliminates the friction of store asset creation. It provides a native, high-performance canvas editor where developers can select curated templates, customize frames and copy, organize screenshot series in collections, and export all required Android and iOS dimensions in a single tap.

## Positioning

"Screenshot it. Ship it." Aperlo is not a generic graphic design tool or an overbuilt marketing suite. It does one specific job with extreme craft: making store screenshots pixel-perfect, compliant, and ready to upload.

## Operating Context

- **Workflow**: Upload raw app screenshots → pick or customize a layout → tweak typography and device mockups → arrange into release collections → batch export all store sizes.
- **Output Requirements**: Google Play Store (Phone 1080×1920, 7" Tablet 1200×1920, 10" Tablet 1600×2560) and Apple App Store (iPhone 6.9" 1320×2868, iPad 13" 2064×2752).
- **Environment**: Native Android companion app with web preview and direct template deep-linking (`aperlo://template/{id}`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Templates**: 80+ curated layout styles (Minimal, Bold Split, OLED Dark, Gradient Flow, Editorial Typography, Centered).
- **Editor**: 50-step undo/redo, layers panel, element snapping, frame presets, custom palettes, lossless PNG and 4K JPG rendering.
- **Collections**: Multi-screen campaign organization with batch generation.
- **Pricing**: Free to download on Google Play.

## Brand Commitments

- **Typography**: Syne (geometric headlines with distinct editorial weight) + DM Sans (precise, readable body typography) + Syne Mono (telemetry and technical tags).
- **Color Palette**: Forest Emerald (`#1A6B4A`), OLED Obsidian (`#141A14`), Clean Canvas White (`#F5F7F5`), Mint Glow (`#4ADE80`).
- **Tone & Voice**: Direct, maker-first, craft-focused, respectful of developers' time and intelligence. No marketing hyperbole.

## Product Principles

1. **The canvas is the product**: Every UI element outside the editor serves the canvas; controls recede, output shines.
2. **One thing at a time**: Clean visual hierarchy where primary actions are obvious and secondary tools stay unobtrusive.
3. **Quality at micro-scale**: Sub-pixel alignment, optical balance, and responsive performance are non-negotiable.
4. **No unearned decoration**: Dividers, badges, and accents exist only when they aid clarity and comprehension.
5. **High-fidelity output demands high-fidelity input**: The tool itself must feel as polished and rock-solid as the assets it exports.
