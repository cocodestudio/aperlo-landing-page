## Overall Impression

Aperlo delivers an exceptionally polished, focused developer marketing page. It communicates its core value proposition ("Screenshot it. Ship it.") within the first 3 seconds and backs it up with tangible proof through real layout cards, technical store dimensions, and an authentic developer tone of voice.

---

## What's Working

1. **Immediate Value Clarity**: The hero section immediately establishes that Aperlo is a specialized screenshot builder with exact Play Store & App Store resolutions.
2. **Tactile Restraint**: Replacing heavy dark drop-shadows with subtle ambient occlusion (`rgba(20,26,20,0.05)`) and 1px hairline borders (`#DCE0DC`) creates an architectural, tool-first feeling.
3. **Fluid Multi-Device Adaptation**: Seamless desktop grid transforms into responsive mobile stacks, preserving native touch scroll momentum.

---

## Priority Issues

### [P2] Mobile Quick Return to Action
- **What**: On smaller mobile screens, scrolling through 6 sections creates a tall page with no instant "Back to Top" or persistent mobile CTA button.
- **Why it matters**: Users convinced by the manifesto or testimonials must scroll all the way back up to tap the primary Google Play download button.
- **Fix**: Ensure the sticky header's download button remains visible and compact on mobile after scrolling past the hero.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] Interactive Template Preview Expansion
- **What**: The 6 template cards look actionable (`cursor: pointer`), but tapping them currently has only micro-lift without an expanded preview modal.
- **Why it matters**: Prospective users want to inspect the exact typography, layers, and color presets in high resolution before downloading.
- **Fix**: Add a lightweight dialog / lightbox preview showing full-resolution template details on click.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable delight`

### [P3] Fluid Typography Ramp Alignment
- **What**: Hero subtitle and section headings use fluid CSS `clamp()` values whose midpoints slightly diverge from the strict discrete scale in `DESIGN.md`.
- **Why it matters**: Design system linting advisories.
- **Fix**: Align fluid clamp bounds directly with the design tokens.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`
